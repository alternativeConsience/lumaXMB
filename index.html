02 / Get set up

Start with a VPK.

Normal VPK installation works.
The companion app is completely optional.

  1. 01

    Download & copy

    On a homebrew-enabled Vita with VitaShell, copy the installer VPK to ux0:/data/install/ over USB or FTP. Normal drag and drop over USB works.

  2. 02

    Install & open

    Install the VPK in VitaShell, open Test Utility, and choose Set up lumaXMB. The bundled installer adds lumaXMB and its Launch Assistant.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    Launch lumaXMB and follow first-time setup. Choose your look, confirm your library options, and finish the scan.

Using the standalone lumaXMB VPK? Copy it to the Vita, install it in VitaShell, then open its bubble directly.

Optional plugin setup

Choose how long it stays active.

Temporary plugins
For the current session, until reboot. A practical starting point for testing.
Permanent plugins
Configured to remain active across reboots. Use Test Utility to manage or remove the permanent session.

!Keep autoboot off. It is not recommended and may not function in this alpha. Permanent plugins and autoboot are separate options.

03 / On your PC Completely optional

Meet the companion.

A Windows workspace for transfers, themes and your media library. Use it when it helps; lumaXMB works without it.

Install & update

Transfer VPKs over USB or FTP, stage supported updates, or share a paired Wi-Fi update from your PC.

Preview & personalize

Load the Vita’s current look, preview waves and settings, and save named presets back to the console.

Manage & troubleshoot

Add media, manage artwork and library folders, and collect logs into a bundle for an alpha bug report.

↳ To use it: open the Windows app, connect by USB or enter the Vita’s FTP address, then choose the task you need.
